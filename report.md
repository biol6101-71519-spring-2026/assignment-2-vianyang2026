Quality assessment of the paired-end E. coli K-12 sequencing data using MultiQC showed generally high-quality reads. Each file (E. coli K-12 _R1 and E. coli K-12 _R2) contained about 0.1 million reads (roughly 100,000 read pairs), with low duplication levels (3.8% in R1 and 3.6% in R2). The GC content was consistent between R1 and R2 (50% in R1 and 51% in R2). All reads were 301 bp long. FastQC did, however, flag low-level adapter contamination in both reads (0.33% of reads).

Alignment to the E. coli K-12 reference genome with Bowtie2 produced a mapping rate of 70.55% (141,101 of 200,000 reads). While 140,042 reads had both reads mapped, only 12.73% (25,454) were properly paired. Since no reads mapped to different chromosomes, cross-mapping can be ruled out. The low rate of proper pairing may be a quality concern and likely stems from the detected adapter sequences and the long 301 bp read length, which may extend beyond the insert size and interfere with correct read orientation.
